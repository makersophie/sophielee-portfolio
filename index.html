Hi, I'm Sophie.A Product Designer based in nyc.

I started my career at Jubo, designing digital products for the B2B healthcare industry. That experience shaped how I think about design: removing friction between a person and what they're trying to do. It also introduced me to the real consequences of inaccessible design and why inclusive solutions matter in high-stakes contexts.

I'm a current Interaction Design student at SVA, expanding my practice into product design, service design, and assistive technology — with a focus on translating complex problems into accessible solutions for the people who need them most.
